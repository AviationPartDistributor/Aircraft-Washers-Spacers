# Aircraft Washers & Spacers: Precision Hardware for Aircraft Maintenance and MRO

Aircraft washers and spacers are important supporting components used throughout aircraft structures, mechanical assemblies, equipment installations, and maintenance applications. Although they are generally smaller than the components they support, their functions can include distributing fastening loads, maintaining clearances, controlling alignment, establishing proper grip dimensions, and protecting surrounding surfaces. Proper selection is therefore an important part of aircraft hardware procurement.

## Shims, Spacers, and Aircraft Component Alignment

Shims and spacers can play an important role in aircraft assembly alignment. A shim may be used to compensate for a controlled dimensional difference, while a spacer can maintain a defined gap or separation between components. These parts can therefore contribute to accurate positioning without necessarily being primary structural fasteners.

In maintenance environments, dimensional accuracy is particularly important because an incorrectly sized spacer or shim can alter the position of connected components. Changes in spacing can affect clearance, alignment, preload, movement, or the relationship between mating parts. For this reason, replacement hardware should be selected according to the applicable aircraft maintenance documentation.

## Materials and Specifications for Aircraft Washers and Spacers

Aircraft washers and spacers can be manufactured from different materials depending on their mechanical, environmental, and installation requirements. Aluminum alloys, steel, stainless steel, titanium, and other engineering materials may be encountered in aviation hardware. The material can influence weight, strength, corrosion resistance, wear characteristics, and compatibility with adjacent components.

Surface treatment and finish may also form part of the component specification. A washer or spacer installed against a particular aircraft structure may require a specific finish or material to address corrosion, wear, electrical characteristics, or other application considerations. Consequently, replacing a component with one that has similar dimensions but a different material or finish may not provide an equivalent result.

Aircraft parts procurement teams should therefore record the complete identification of washers and spacers whenever possible. Part number, standard, dimensions, material, finish, quantity, and intended application can all help ensure that the sourced component corresponds with the aircraft requirement.

## Aircraft Washers and Spacers for MRO Procurement

MRO organizations can require washers and spacers during structural repairs, component replacement, equipment installation, inspections, refurbishment, and routine aircraft maintenance. Some requirements may involve common hardware that is regularly stocked, while others can involve specialized components associated with a particular aircraft platform or assembly.

Because washers and spacers are often installed together with larger components, they can sometimes be overlooked during procurement planning. However, replacing an assembly without accounting for its associated washers, shims, spacers, or other supporting hardware can create an incomplete maintenance requirement. Proper inventory management therefore benefits from treating these components as distinct aircraft parts with their own identification and specifications.

Aircraft washers and spacers provide essential support across many aircraft systems and structures. From load distribution and fastening support to alignment, clearance, and dimensional control, these components contribute to the proper installation of larger assemblies. Accurate part identification, specification matching, dimensional verification, and dependable aviation hardware sourcing can help MRO organizations manage these requirements more effectively.
